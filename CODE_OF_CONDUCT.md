# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in Agentic Git Janitor.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Communicate respectfully
- Give constructive, specific feedback
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them
- Protect private and security-sensitive information

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation
- Discriminatory or demeaning language
- Personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions
- Encouraging unsafe or malicious use of the project

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces directly connected to the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
